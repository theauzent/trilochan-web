Refund & Cancellation Policy

At Trilochan Manpower Supply, we value our clients' satisfaction. Our refund policy is as follows:

1. Cancellation

Service requests can be cancelled within 24 hours of booking for a full refund. Cancellations after 24 hours may incur a small processing fee.

2. Refunds

If the assigned worker does not report for duty, a replacement or full refund will be provided. Refunds are processed within 5-7 working days to the original payment method.

3. Non-Refundable

Registration fees (if any) are non-refundable once the account is activated.

4. Dispute Resolution

In case of any payment disputes, please reach out to our support team immediately.
